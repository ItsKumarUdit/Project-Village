Bhitiharwa Ashram

Track Location More Images

Bhitiharwa Ashram, located in Thana Gaunaha, West Champaran, Bihar (Pin-845455), is a historic place associated with Mahatma Gandhi’s Champaran Satyagraha of 1917. Gandhi chose this ashram as one of the centers for spreading education and creating social awareness among the oppressed farmers who were forced to cultivate indigo by the British. It also served as a base for training volunteers in cleanliness, education, and self-reliance. Today, the ashram stands as a symbol of India’s freedom struggle and attracts tourists and history enthusiasts who wish to connect with Gandhi’s legacy and Champaran’s historic role in India’s independence movement.

Gandhi Ashram Park

Track Location More Images

Park at Bhithiharwa Ashram in West Champaran, Bihar, is a peaceful spot that holds both natural beauty and historical value. Surrounded by greenery, the park provides a calm environment for visitors to relax and connect with nature. The Ashram itself is linked to Mahatma Gandhi’s stay during the freedom movement, making the place culturally and historically significant. People often visit this park not only for leisure but also to reflect on the values of simplicity and truth that Gandhi promoted. With its blend of history, spirituality, and natural charm, the park at Bhithiharwa Ashram is an inspiring destination for locals and tourists alike.

Bhikhna Thori

Track Location More Images

Thori, also known as Bhikhna Thori, is a beautiful and lesser-known destination on the Bihar–Nepal border in West Champaran. The place is surrounded by dense forests, gentle hills, and calm rivers, creating a refreshing and peaceful atmosphere. On clear winter mornings, visitors can even catch a glimpse of the distant Himalayan ranges, which add a magical charm to the horizon. The region is home to attractions like Lakshman Jhula, Sita Gufa, and lakeside spots where travelers can relax amidst nature. Its untouched beauty, serene environment, and cultural significance make Bakna Thori a hidden treasure for tourists, adventure seekers, and history lovers alike.

The Sahodra Mela in West Champaran, Bihar, is a famous cultural and religious fair held every year during Chaitra Navratri. The fair centers around the Subhadra Mata Temple, where devotees from Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, and Nepal gather to offer prayers. It is especially important for the Tharu community, who come together for worship, cultural exchange, and social discussions. The mela also features swings, rides, and shops, creating a festive atmosphere. Every year, lakhs of devotees attend, making it a grand event. With strong security arrangements, the Sahodra Mela continues to represent faith, unity, and tradition in the region.

Sofa Temple

Track Location More Images

The Sofa Temple, or Sofa Shiva Temple, near Thori on the Bihar–Nepal border, is an ancient shrine built on a 70-foot mound surrounded by forests and rivers. Archaeological remains, including idols of Durga, Vishnu, and Shiva from the 9th–12th centuries, highlight its historic value. Local legend claims a hidden cave beneath the temple leads to Patal Lok, created by Lord Vishwakarma. During Sawan, devotees perform jalābhishek on the Shivling, praying for rain and prosperity. Protected by embankments, the temple remains a symbol of Champaran’s faith and heritage.

Rampurva Ashokan Pillars

Track Location More Images

Nestled in Rampurva village of West Champaran, Bihar—just shy of the Nepal border—stand the remains of two unique Ashokan Pillars, erected by Emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE. One pillar bears a lion capital inscribed with the Major Pillar Edicts I–VI, while its twin, about 300 meters away, features an intricately crafted bull capital. Astoundingly, the lion capital now resides in Kolkata's Indian Museum and the bull in New Delhi’s Rashtrapati Bhavan. Tradition links the site to Buddha’s renunciation on the banks of the Harbora River, making it both a sacred Buddhist landmark and a testament to Mauryan artistry and legacy.

Solar Plant

Track Location More Images

In Gaunaha, West Champaran, Bihar, a solar power plant has been set up to produce clean electricity. The project, started in 2017, generates about 5 megawatts of power, which is enough to light up many homes in nearby villages. This solar plant helps reduce the use of coal and diesel, making the environment cleaner. It also supports farmers and households by giving them access to reliable electricity. Bihar is working on many such solar projects, including rooftop panels and solar pumps. The plant in Gaunaha shows how renewable energy can improve life in rural areas and promote green living.
